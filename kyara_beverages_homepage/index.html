About Us

At Kyara Beverages, we’re reimagining refreshment with millet based fruit drinks. Rooted in tradition and powered by innovation, we’re on a mission to craft drinks that are nutritious, refreshing, and real.

Popular Drinks

Blackberry Lime Porch Punch

Blackberry-Lime Porch Punch is an extremely refreshingly sweet beverage that combines the juicy flavors of blackberries with the taste of lime and the spicy kick of ginger beer.

Peach Lemonade

This fun, fruity, and refreshing peach lemonade is perfect for serving to family and friends. Serve over ice with water, soda water for a delicious summer sipper!

Frozen Raspberry Lemonade

Closer to a smoothie in texture, you’ll savor each sip of this pink drink. It’s tartness is balanced by sweetness imparted by the natural addition of maple sugar.
